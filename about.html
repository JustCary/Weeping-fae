About the Artisan

Handmade adornments inspired by Renaissance, medieval, fantasy, and historical styles.

The Weeping Fae

The Weeping Fae is a handmade jewelry shop focused on creating Renaissance-inspired, medieval, fantasy, and historically influenced accessories.

Each piece is made by hand rather than mass-produced, allowing individual variations and craftsmanship to become part of the character of every adornment.

Handcrafted With Intention

The collection includes glasses chains, decorative bells, earrings, hair pins, and chainmail necklaces.

Many pieces are produced in small quantities, making availability limited compared with commercially manufactured jewelry.

Inspiration

The designs draw inspiration from Renaissance courts, medieval ornamentation, folklore, fantasy worlds, historical accessories, chainmail techniques, and decorative metalwork.
