Frequently Asked Questions

Are the pieces handmade?

Yes. The jewelry and accessories are handcrafted rather than mass-produced, so small variations may appear between individual pieces.

Why are quantities limited?

Handmade items take time to create, and many designs may only be available in quantities of one or two.

What types of jewelry are available?

The collection may include glasses chains, decorative bells, earrings, hair pins, chainmail necklaces, and other Renaissance-inspired accessories.

Can I request custom jewelry?

Custom jewelry requests may be added as a future feature. Potential options could include different colors, metals, chain lengths, charms, or decorative elements.

Can I purchase sold-out products?

Sold-out products cannot currently be added to the shopping cart.
